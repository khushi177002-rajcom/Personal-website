My Favourite Recipe: Veg Sandwich

Ingredients

  • Bread slices
  • Tomato
  • Cucumber
  • Onion
  • Butter
  • Green chutney

Preparation Steps

  1. Take two bread slices.
  2. Apply butter and green chutney.
  3. Add sliced vegetables.
  4. Place the other bread slice on top.
  5. Cut the sandwich and serve.
